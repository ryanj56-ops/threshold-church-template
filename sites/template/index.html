Who We Are

Grace Fellowship Church

We are a Spirit-filled, Bible-believing church committed to reaching our community with the love of Jesus Christ. Founded on the principles of discipleship, worship, and outreach, our congregation spans generations and backgrounds — united by a common faith and a shared calling to serve.

Love

Loving God and loving people is the foundation of everything we do.

Truth

We are anchored in Scripture and committed to teaching God's Word faithfully.

Community

Life is better together. We build real relationships that last.
